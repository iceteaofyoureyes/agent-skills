PetClinic, Appointment, CR-DWC, and old phase labels remain in their dated benchmark/example scope. Use neutral VNext examples for current operator learning.

## Historical path and username policy

Raw historical benchmark transcripts remain unchanged where their exact bytes are evidence. Their machine-local paths/usernames are historical capture data, not reusable operator paths or supported prerequisites. New current documentation must not copy machine-local paths. Any shareable projection derived from raw evidence must redact usernames and home paths while preserving stable repository-relative paths, commit/tree identifiers, and content hashes. Keep the raw record and redacted projection linked; do not silently rewrite raw evidence.
